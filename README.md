# Retail Marketing Intelligence System

## Project Overview

An end-to-end retail analytics and data science project using dunnhumby's "The Complete Journey" dataset.

The project aims to understand customer purchasing behaviour, evaluate marketing campaign performance, and develop predictive models to support more effective customer targeting.

## Business Problem

Retailers invest in promotions and personalised marketing campaigns, but customer participation and coupon redemption do not necessarily indicate incremental business value.

This project investigates how purchasing behaviour changes around marketing campaigns, which households are more likely to respond, and how analytical insights can inform future marketing decisions.

## Key Research Questions

1. How do customer purchasing patterns vary across households and over time?
2. Are marketing campaigns associated with changes in customer spending and engagement?
3. Which customer characteristics predict campaign response?
4. How can customer targeting strategies be improved?

## Dataset

**Source** dunnhumby - The Complete Journey

https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/frtgnn/dunnhumby-the-complete-journey

The dataset represents approximately two years of household-level retail transactions from 2,500 households, with customer attributes and marketing contact history available for selected households.

## Important Considerations

The dataset is a representation of retail shopping activity. Observational associations should not automatically be interpreted as causal effects.

Campaign profitability and incremental impact will only be estimated where the available data and methodological assumptions support them.
